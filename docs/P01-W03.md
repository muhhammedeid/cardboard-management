# P01-W03 — Purchase Invoice and Stock Integration

## Business mapping

Submitting a `Cardboard Supply` creates and submits one standard ERPNext
`Purchase Invoice` with `update_stock = 1`. It has exactly one item row:

```text
qty            = net_weight
stock_qty      = net_weight
rate           = total_amount / net_weight
amount         = total_amount
warehouse      = Cardboard Supply.warehouse
item_code      = Cardboard Supply.item
uom/stock_uom  = Kg
```

The supplier discount changes only `payable_weight` and `total_amount`. Physical
inventory always receives `net_weight`. ERPNext posts the stock ledger,
valuation, supplier payable, and GL entries; this app creates no Purchase
Receipt, Stock Entry, custom GL entry, or Payment Entry.

## Deterministic integration and idempotency

The company and currency are derived from the selected Warehouse. The server
requires an enabled Supplier, an enabled non-group Warehouse with a valid
Company, and an enabled stock Item whose stock UOM is Kg.

`Cardboard Supply.purchase_invoice` stores the generated invoice. The standard
Purchase Invoice receives a unique, read-only `custom_cardboard_supply` backlink.
A row lock plus both links make retries idempotent. A valid existing draft is
completed, a valid submitted invoice is reused, and a cancelled, missing, or
mismatched invoice is rejected instead of duplicated.

## Precision

`Purchase Invoice Item.rate` uses nine-decimal precision so repeating effective
rates can preserve the exact currency total. Item amount and invoice grand total
continue to follow ERPNext/company currency precision. Creation aborts if the
standard ERPNext calculation cannot reproduce `Cardboard Supply.total_amount`.

The custom field and rate precision Property Setter are installed idempotently
by `cardboard_management.setup.ensure_purchase_invoice_integration_schema` from
both `after_install` and `after_migrate`. No ERPNext or Frappe source schema is
modified.

## Cancellation

Before Cardboard Supply cancellation, its forward link is temporarily cleared,
the submitted Purchase Invoice is cancelled, and the link is restored. The
operation is one database transaction: failure rolls back both documents.
Standard ERPNext cancellation reverses stock and GL effects.

## Manual example evidence

Example supply `CS-2026-00003` generated Purchase Invoice
`ACC-PINV-2026-00001`:

- net weight / PI qty / stock qty / Stock Ledger quantity: `900 Kg`
- payable weight: `850 Kg`
- source rate per Kg: `6.50 EGP`
- effective PI rate: `6.138888889 EGP`
- PI item amount / grand total / supplier payable: `5,525.00 EGP`
- item rows: `1`; `update_stock = 1`; both documents submitted

After cancelling the Cardboard Supply, both documents had `docstatus = 2`, net
Stock Ledger quantity was `0`, active GL entry count was `0`, and Bin quantity
returned from `900` to `0`.
