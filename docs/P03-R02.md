# P03-R02 — Custom Supplier Payment Operational UX

## Scope

`Cardboard Supplier Payment` is an app-owned, submittable operational wrapper
for paying suppliers. The user fills business fields only (supplier, amount,
mode of payment, posting date, optional references/notes). On submit the server
generates a standard ERPNext Payment Entry natively, validates its mapping, and
submits it. The wrapper stores only the `payment_entry` link and read-only
display state; ERPNext remains the sole ledger, outstanding, and balance
authority. The Cardboard Supply payment path is unchanged.

## Company resolution

No Company field is exposed. The payment company is resolved server-side from
the supplier's submitted, outstanding Purchase Invoices: exactly one distinct
company must qualify, otherwise submit is rejected with a clear error. This is
configuration-derived (ERPNext data), never guessed from defaults.

## Payment Entry generation

1. Wrapper is validated (supplier enabled, amount > 0 and <= total outstanding,
   enabled Mode of Payment).
2. Company is resolved from the supplier's outstanding Purchase Invoices
   (FIFO by posting date, then name).
3. The Cash/Bank account comes from ERPNext's native Mode of Payment Account
   mapping (`erpnext...sales_invoice.get_bank_cash_account`) - the same source
   `get_payment_entry` uses internally. It must belong to the resolved company,
   be enabled, non-group, Cash or Bank, and use the company currency.
4. `erpnext.accounts.doctype.payment_entry.payment_entry.get_payment_entry`
   builds the unsaved native Payment Entry against the oldest outstanding
   Purchase Invoice. Only that unsaved draft is adjusted: references are
   reallocated FIFO across the supplier's outstanding invoices, paid/received
   amounts set to the wrapper amount, reference no/date and remarks mapped.
5. The draft is validated (company/party/amount/posting date/mode of payment),
   natively inserted and submitted, then re-validated. Only the
   `payment_entry` link is persisted on the wrapper.

Idempotency follows the Quick Expense precedent: a per-wrapper row lock plus a
backlink lookup (`Payment Entry.cardboard_supplier_payment`) make concurrent or
retry submissions complete the existing Payment Entry instead of creating a
duplicate.

## Cancellation

Wrapper cancel cancels its linked native Payment Entry (after validating the
mapping) so Purchase Invoice outstanding amounts are restored by ERPNext
itself. If the Payment Entry was cancelled externally, the wrapper stays
read-only and reports `Cancelled`. No outstanding amounts, ledgers, or balances
are ever written by the custom app.

## Permissions

The wrapper uses only the standard `Accounts User` and `Accounts Manager`
roles - the same authority boundary as native Payment Entry. No Payment Entry
permissions are granted or modified, and `setup.py` / Property Setters are not
touched (the wrapper is a first-class DocType, not a schema extension).

## Workspace and translations

The `New Supplier Payment` quick-action shortcut is repointed from Payment
Entry to Cardboard Supplier Payment in both the committed workspace JSON and
the deterministic `content_builder.py`; the `Payments` list shortcut stays on
Payment Entry. The route allowlist in `public/js/cardboard_management.js` is
extended only with `Cardboard Supplier Payment`, and its primary action is
relabeled to the supported, translated `Save and Submit Payment`
(`حفظ واعتماد الدفعة`). All new user-facing labels are translated in
`translations/ar.csv`.
