# P02-W03 — Operational Dashboard

## Scope

P02-W03 adds a management dashboard directly to the public **Cardboard Management** Desk workspace. It uses standard Frappe Number Cards and Dashboard Charts backed by small app-owned aggregate query helpers. It does not add a BI framework, summary table, scheduler, custom stock balance, custom supplier balance, or ledger.

All source and metadata remain inside `cardboard_management`. Frappe and ERPNext core are unchanged.

## Deterministic scope

The app now owns a Single DocType named **Cardboard Dashboard Settings** with two required fields:

- **Company** — scopes every KPI and chart.
- **Cardboard Item Group** — scopes current stock and purchases-by-type to the configured Item Group subtree through ERPNext Item Group nested-set bounds.

No company, Item Group, Item, Supplier, or Warehouse name is hard-coded in the dashboard service.

On `cardboard.localhost`, the settings were configured to the existing site values:

- Company: `El Nos`
- Cardboard Item Group: `Used Cardboard`

These are site configuration values, not source-code constants.

## KPI cards

### Today

1. **Today's Supplies Weight** — sum of submitted `Cardboard Supply.net_weight` for today, restricted through the selected Warehouse's company.
2. **Today's Purchase Amount** — sum of submitted `Cardboard Supply.total_amount` for today and company.
3. **Today's Supplier Payments** — sum of standard `Payment Entry Reference.allocated_amount` for submitted Pay/Supplier Payment Entries dated today whose reference is a Purchase Invoice carrying the app's Cardboard Supply backlink.
4. **Today's Expenses** — sum of submitted `Quick Expense.amount` for today and company.

### Current

5. **Current Stock Weight** — sum of standard ERPNext `Bin.actual_qty`, joined to Warehouse company and Item/Item Group scope. Cardboard Supply history is not used to reconstruct stock.
6. **Supplier Outstanding** — sum of positive per-supplier balances from standard ERPNext `Payment Ledger Entry` state for the configured company, Supplier parties, and Payable accounts through today. No supplier balance is stored by the app.

### This month

7. **This Month Purchased Weight** — submitted Cardboard Supply net weight in the current calendar month.
8. **This Month Purchase Cost** — submitted Cardboard Supply total amount in the current calendar month.
9. **This Month Expenses** — submitted Quick Expense amount in the current calendar month.
10. **This Month Paid to Suppliers** — standard Payment Entry allocations to app-linked Cardboard Supply Purchase Invoices in the current calendar month.

Using allocation rows rather than the full Payment Entry amount prevents overcounting when one payment covers both cardboard and unrelated purchases.

## Charts

- **Daily Supplied Weight** — exactly 30 calendar days including today; one grouped query plus zero-filled missing dates.
- **Purchases by Cardboard Type** — current-month submitted supply weight grouped by Item and restricted to the configured Cardboard Item Group subtree.
- **Top Suppliers** — current-month submitted supply weight grouped by Supplier, ordered descending, limited to 10.
- **Supplier Outstanding** — current positive payable balance grouped by Supplier from Payment Ledger Entry, ordered descending, limited to 10.

Each chart is a standard app-owned `Dashboard Chart` using an app-owned custom `Dashboard Chart Source`. Source functions share the tested aggregate service and require Cardboard Supply read permission.

## Workspace layout

The top of the workspace is now ordered for operations:

1. Quick Actions: New Cardboard Supply, New Expense, Suppliers, Payment Entries.
2. TODAY: four Number Cards.
3. CURRENT: two Number Cards.
4. THIS MONTH: four Number Cards.
5. Charts: four two-column charts.
6. Existing operational list shortcuts, cards, inventory shortcuts, and report links.
7. Coming Soon: Scale Integration only.

Existing Cardboard Supply, Quick Expense, Supplier, Payment Entry, Item, Warehouse, and report destinations remain accessible.

## Performance

- All transaction metrics use SQL aggregation; rows are not loaded into Python.
- No N+1 queries are used.
- Cardboard Supply and Quick Expense queries are date-bounded for today/month/chart workloads.
- Added app-owned physical indexes:
  - Cardboard Supply: `posting_date`, `supplier`, `item`, `warehouse`
  - Quick Expense: `posting_date`, `company`
- ERPNext's existing indexes are used for Bin warehouse/item lookup, Payment Entry Reference joins, Payment Ledger Entry company/date/party filtering, Item Group nested-set scope, and primary-key joins.
- Representative `EXPLAIN` plans were reviewed. On the tiny live tables MySQL may choose a full scan for five Cardboard Supply rows; the date and join indexes are available and were verified in `information_schema.statistics` for production-sized tables.
- Chart sources use Frappe's standard Dashboard Chart source cache.
