# P01-W04 — Supplier Payment Flow

## Scope

Cardboard Supply now initiates supplier payments through the standard ERPNext
Payment Entry. It does not create custom ledgers, supplier balances, payment
tables, or a second payment-status source of truth.

## User flow

On a submitted Cardboard Supply whose linked Purchase Invoice is submitted and
has a positive outstanding amount, **Record Payment** is shown.

1. The user selects an enabled non-group Cash or Bank account belonging to the
   linked Purchase Invoice company.
2. The server reloads the saved Cardboard Supply, validates its submitted state,
   link, backlink, supplier, invoice status, outstanding amount, and selected
   account.
3. ERPNext `get_payment_entry("Purchase Invoice", ...)` builds a standard,
   unsaved Payment Entry.
4. The form opens for review. ERPNext maps `payment_type = Pay`, Supplier party,
   company, posting date, supplier payable account, cash/bank account, and one
   Purchase Invoice reference with the current outstanding allocation.
5. The user may reduce paid/received/allocated amounts for a partial payment and
   submits manually. The custom app never auto-submits a Payment Entry.

ERPNext's standard outgoing-payment convention is used: `paid_from` is the
selected Cash/Bank account (credited), while `paid_to` is the supplier payable
account (debited).

## Legacy payable-weight display (P01-W04A)

Development-era records created before the P01-W02 schema may store
`payable_weight = 0` while `net_weight > 0` and `total_amount > 0`. For those
records the correct payable weight for display is the recorded `net_weight`.
`display_payable_weight` is a virtual read-only field: it returns the recorded
`payable_weight` when set, otherwise `net_weight`. Stored weight values, the
derived PDF/print fields, and submitted accounting history are never rewritten.

## Derived display


`purchase_invoice_outstanding` and `payment_status` are read-only virtual fields.
They are refreshed from the linked submitted Purchase Invoice in `onload` and are
not independent persisted accounting state.

- `outstanding <= 0` → **Paid**
- `outstanding >= grand_total` → **Unpaid**
- otherwise → **Partially Paid**

Reloading Cardboard Supply after Payment Entry submission or cancellation always
reflects ERPNext's current outstanding amount.

## Validation and security

Payment initiation rejects draft supplies, missing/draft/cancelled invoices,
invalid backlinks, supplier mismatch, fully paid invoices, and invalid or
cross-company Cash/Bank accounts. The whitelisted controller method reloads the
saved Cardboard Supply before validation so client-supplied document values are
not authoritative. Standard ERPNext Payment Entry validation prevents allocation
above current outstanding.

## Integration state (P01-W04A)

`integration_status`, `invoice_total`, `invoice_paid_amount`,
`purchase_invoice_outstanding`, and `payment_status` are virtual read-only fields
refreshed in `onload` from the linked Purchase Invoice:

- No linked submitted invoice → `Not Integrated`; no outstanding is shown, so a
  missing integration is never presented as `0` / Paid.
- Cancelled or mismatched linked invoice → `Invalid Link`.
- Valid linked submitted invoice → `Integrated` plus
  `invoice_total = grand_total`, `invoice_paid_amount = grand_total -
  outstanding_amount`, outstanding, and the derived payment status.

The client shows an ERPNext Integration dashboard headline (Not Integrated /
Invalid Link / Integrated / Integrated - Paid / Integrated - Partially Paid) and
keeps Record Payment visible only for a submitted supply with a valid submitted
linked invoice and outstanding > 0.
